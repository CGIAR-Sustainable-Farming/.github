# CGIAR Sustainable Farming

The **CGIAR Sustainable Farming Program** brings together agronomy, plant health and farming-system research to help smallholder farmers improve productivity, profitability and resilience while protecting natural resources.

Part of CGIAR's 2025–2030 research portfolio, the program develops integrated farm-management solutions with farmers, national research and extension systems, governments, research institutions, civil society and the private sector. Its work prioritizes women and youth and includes soil health, water and nutrient management, plant health, and evidence for sustainable farming decisions.

[Explore the program](https://www.cgiar.org/cgiar-research-portfolio-2025-2030/sustainable-farming)

## About this GitHub organization

This organization hosts research software, data workflows and analytical tools for Sustainable Farming, alongside resources developed through Excellence in Agronomy. It is also the intended home for the SFP data hub, currently in preparation.

Explore the [repositories](https://github.com/orgs/CGIAR-Sustainable-Farming/repositories) and the [Evidence for Resilient Agriculture (ERAg) resource](https://cgiar-sustainable-farming.github.io/ERAg/). Each repository documents its own purpose, status, contributors and licence.

## Our history

This GitHub organization was previously named **EiA2030**, supporting CGIAR's **Excellence in Agronomy** initiative. On **6 November 2026**, it was renamed **CGIAR-Sustainable-Farming** to support the broader Sustainable Farming program while retaining existing repositories and their development history.

The program builds on work from **Excellence in Agronomy**, **Plant Health**, and **Mixed Farming Systems**. [Read CGIAR's account of this transition](https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/sustainable-farming-cultivating-a-water-wise-future).

Legacy project names and acknowledgements remain part of the research record. The organization rename does not change the authorship or licensing of individual outputs.
